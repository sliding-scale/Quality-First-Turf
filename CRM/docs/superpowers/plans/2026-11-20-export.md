# Export data Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:executing-plans. Steps use checkbox syntax.

**Goal:** The export of clients, projects (jobs included), quotes, invoices and payments (phases.md, Milestone 5): one Settings page that downloads each as a CSV, so the business can always take its data out (and so Jobber's end-of-life has a way back).

**Architecture:** `convex/crm/exports.ts` has one paginated query, `page({businessId, kind, cursor})`, that returns a page of ready-to-write rows (names, not ids) and the next cursor. The page loops until it is done and builds the file in the browser. Each kind needs the view level of its own module (clients, requests, quotes, invoices, payments) and is refused to a person limited to records assigned to them. Cells are made safe for spreadsheets (a text cell that starts with `=`, `@` or looks like a formula gets a leading apostrophe).

**Spec:** D-101 in `CRM/docs/decisions.md`. Assumptions (D-091): money in dollars with two decimals, dates as UTC `YYYY-MM-DD`, one file per kind, whole business (not filtered), Settings > Export data like Jobber's.

## Global Constraints
- Additive only; code in `convex/crm/`; no hyphens in file names; pure CSV rules in `convex/crm/lib/exportCsv.ts`.
- Costs are not exported here (no price lists); invoices, quotes and payments are money records and follow their own module.

## Review Focus
- A formula-looking cell is neutralised, but a phone like `+1 555 0100` and a number like `-5` are left alone.
- Quotes, commas and line breaks inside cells are escaped.
- A person limited to assigned records, and a person with no access to a module, cannot export it.
- Another business's rows never appear; a deleted client does not break a page.
- Paging gives every row exactly once.
