## Third pass (2026-11-20): the process documents and the examples folder
- **Sales pipeline process:** new lead (the rep records name, phone, contact type, email, address and lead source; a company is created first if the lead is one; then a deal is made with the company, contact and project address), schedule on-site consultation, (steps 3 and 4 not read), deliver proposal, proposal sent and pending decision, finalize contract and signature, closed won, closed lost. Automations follow up the quote; before the project starts the project manager walks the job with the salesperson and is responsible for marking out the area and any electrical or water sources. This matches our Sales stages.
- **Construction process:** pending to start, PM job walk and mark-up, started, 20%, 40%, 60%, 80%, 100%, PM job walk with client, correction needed, completed. Ours (D-080) has no separate "Started" or "100%" stage and ends at "100% Completed & Sign-off"; the PRD's list was followed. Differences only.
- **Pre-planning process:** 14 steps (see above); steps 7 to 10 were not read.
- **Accounts payable and receivable reporting process:** every Tuesday the bookkeeper sends four reports: payable and receivable for QFT Landscape, and payable and receivable for QFT Construction and Quality First Patios. The payable review keeps only what has to be paid (pending material bills, labor payroll, office payroll), drops what is paid but not yet through the bank, lists pending **sales commissions** in their own section, and says which account to pay each bill from. The receivable review checks payments are applied to the right invoices and the amounts are right. The four reports go to the owner on Thursday and payments are followed up. **The CRM has receivable (invoices, payments) but no payable side (bills, payroll, commissions) and no weekly report.**
- **Document Examples folder:** other companies' proposals and guides kept as references (System Pavers examples, AGL, Planting Design Diagram, Plan Examples Bella Terra, Moon Valley and its in-home consultation process, StruXure partnership programs), the proposal exhibits (Exhibit 1 All Inclusive, Exhibit 2 Plants and 2 3x3 pots, Exhibit 3 Irrigation) and a sprinkler installation guide.
