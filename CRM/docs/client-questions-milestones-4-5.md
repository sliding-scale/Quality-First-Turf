# Quality First Turf CRM: a few things we need from you

These are the only items we cannot work out ourselves. Everything else we have set up sensibly and you can change later. Real examples are better than descriptions. Customer names can be removed. "I don't know yet" is a fine answer: tell us who would know.

## 1. Material formulas and wastage
We are building the form that turns site measurements into a material list. To make it calculate the way you do, please send:
- How you work out the quantity of each material from the measurements (for example, turf from the area, base rock from the area and depth, infill, nails or edging from the perimeter).
- The wastage you allow, either one percentage for everything or a different one for each material or job type.
- **2 or 3 real jobs** showing the site measurements and the final material list you ordered. We will run them through the form and check that we get the same list as you did.

## 2. An example work package
Please send a recent work package (or whatever you give an installer before a job), and tell us what one normally contains: for example the address, scope of work, drawings, photos, material list, schedule, access notes, and what the installer is paid. Also tell us if there is anything the installer must not see, such as the customer's price.

## 3. Business texting registration (please send this soon)
US mobile carriers must approve a business before it can send text messages, and approval can take several weeks, so we would like to start it early. We need:
- Legal business name
- EIN (tax ID)
- Business address
- Website

## 4. At launch: phone numbers and WhatsApp
We do not need these yet. We will ask again when we get close to launch.
- Which business phone numbers should be in the CRM, and should we keep your current numbers or use new ones?
- Access to your WhatsApp Business account (Meta Business admin access) and the number you want to use for it. That number cannot already be registered on regular WhatsApp.
